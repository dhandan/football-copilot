## What we learned

GW1 produced five correct 1X2 outcomes from ten matches.  This is broadly close to the historical Model 2 accuracy of 52.69%, but ten matches are far too few to draw firm conclusions about model performance.

Four areas are now being monitored through GW5:

1. **xG compression** — the model may be pulling team-strength estimates too strongly towards league-average scoring levels.
2. **1-1 modal scoreline concentration** — eight of ten fixtures had 1-1 as the single most likely scoreline, while none actually finished 1-1.
3. **Promoted-team pessimism** — the cold-start framework underestimated Hull and Ipswich in GW1.
4. **Upset calibration** — lower-probability results will be tracked to determine whether GW1 was normal football variance or a systematic bias.

5. **Incremental predictive value** — Model 2 will be compared with simple historical benchmarks to determine whether its fixture-specific features consistently improve prediction quality.

No changes will be made to Model 2 during the initial five-Gameweek live monitoring period.
