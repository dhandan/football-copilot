**Adaptive-confidence challenger:** The registered `AdaptiveMarketConfidence_v1.0` forecast was frozen on 23 October 2026 at 09:43 UTC, before the GW8 fixtures. Its weights vary with bookmaker confidence under the pre-registered rule in `data/live/challenger_predictions/2026_27_gw08_adaptive_confidence_v1.json`, rather than using constant 60% market / 40% Model 2 weights. Scoring its ten frozen `data/live/challenger_predictions/2026_27_gw08_adaptive_confidence_v1.csv` probability rows against the ten verified GW8 results gives **3/10 correct (30%), mean Log Loss 1.1981, mean multiclass Brier 0.7415**. This calculation was performed directly from the frozen file; no separate adaptive evaluation CSV has yet been committed. The adaptive approach improved the fixed blend's Log Loss by about **0.0040** and Brier by about **0.0031**, with **no improvement in 1X2 accuracy**. These differences are marginal and do not support promotion on their own.

Model 2 was evaluated with `python scripts/evaluate_gameweek.py --gameweek 8`, yielding 3/10, Log Loss 1.343910 and Brier 0.839573. The blend was evaluated with `python scripts/evaluate_market_shadow.py --gameweek 8`, yielding 3/10, Log Loss 1.2021 and Brier 0.7446. Baselines were evaluated locally with `python scripts/evaluate_baselines.py --gameweek 8`. Market-only scores were separately calculated from the frozen GW8 market probabilities and final results, using normalised H/D/A probabilities; **market-only scores have not yet been saved as a distinct evaluation CSV**.

The market correctly selected Arsenal, Fulham, Newcastle **and Liverpool**, whereas Model 2 and the blend selected Arsenal, Fulham and Newcastle. All three methods failed to select any of the three drawn fixtures; Model 2 had zero draw picks. The market had the best GW8 accuracy and Log Loss among the market-only method and three model variants, while the historical outcome-frequency baseline had a slightly lower Brier (0.6903 versus market 0.6918).
