## Promoted-team cold start

Some promoted teams do not yet have sufficient current Premier League history for the standard feature pipeline.

Football Copilot therefore uses a validated promoted-team cold-start prior.

The current methodology combines:

- **75% translated Championship performance**
- **25% historical Premier League baseline**

The weighting was selected using a historical promoted-team backtest.

## A modelling distinction worth noting

The predicted outcome and the most likely individual scoreline do not always agree.

The outcome probability is the combined probability of every scoreline producing that result, while the most likely scoreline refers to one individual score combination.
