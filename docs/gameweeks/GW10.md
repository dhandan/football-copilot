### Registered diagnostics and decisions

- **H01 blend validation:** Continue the fixed 40/60 as the unchanged *provisional primary candidate*, keeping Model 2 as the benchmark and recording paired GW11 evidence. **No formal promotion** without registered threshold, uncertainty, operating-role switch and rollback review.
- **Market source audit (H02):** GW10 10/10 fixture parity and valid no-vig UK bookmaker probabilities passed. 17–18 complete bookmaker prices per fixture (versus 20–21 in GW9); bookmaker-panel variation is a cross-week comparability limitation. The Everton fixture was frozen only 10.33 hours before kickoff; the T−24h exception is retained, not passed.
- **Draw and modal score calibration:** Two actual draws (Fulham–Newcastle and Man United–Villa), zero 1X2 draw argmax selections by any method; Model 2 had seven modal `1-1` scorelines and **one correct exact score** (Fulham–Newcastle). Investigate draw-probability calibration and exact-score mode separately; do not post-hoc inflate draw probability.
- **Model/market disagreement:** Leeds–Tottenham was the *only* GW10 argmax disagreement. Model 2 and blends chose Leeds correctly, market-only chose Tottenham incorrectly. A one-fixture success is not enough to fit or select weights.
- **Cold starts:** Coventry City, Hull City and Ipswich were the three GW10 affected opponent teams, with Model 2 **3/3** correct 1X2 outcomes on those fixtures. Insufficient segment sample to infer robust cold-start performance.
- **Surprises:** All four methods missed Nott'm Forest beating Man City and Sunderland beating Chelsea; keep per-fixture Log Loss/Brier contributions, rather than fitting to individual upsets.
- **GW11 operating decision:** No new model or changing blend weights. Continue all four frozen-method series prospectively, fix the FPL status-source discrepancy, register an explicit promotion rule before any future status decision, and keep V2 agent development / £0 cloud exploration separate.

## Focused GW10 experimental questions (proposed diagnostics, not approved new contenders)
1. **Draw probability / calibration.** All four GW9 methods missed the two drawn fixtures by argmax. Review GW6–GW9 forecast probability assigned to draws, observed draw frequency, draw-specific log loss/Brier contributions, and uncertainty. Use frozen data only. Do not simply increase draw probabilities based on GW9.
2. **Model-versus-market disagreements and cold starts.** GW9 Coventry–Sunderland was won by Model 2 but not by market/blends; examine *all* disagreement and cold-start fixtures on the prospective matched set, with sample sizes and per-match contributions. Do not generalise from two GW9 cold starts.
3. **Modal 1-1 scoreline concentration.** Examine whether ten Model 2 1-1 modal predictions reflect reasonable Poisson mechanics, rounding or a feature/model issue; keep exact-score accuracy separate from 1X2 predictions.

**Experiment gate:** Diagnose first using existing frozen observations. If evidence motivates a new probabilistic method, write a falsifiable hypothesis, formula, minimum sample, prospective acceptance/guardrails and the intended pre-match release time **before** freezing its first shadow. Do not add arbitrary last-minute variants. Current recommendation is to carry forward the four unchanged approaches and avoid a new GW10 weight experiment.
