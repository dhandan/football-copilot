## Focused GW10 experimental questions (proposed diagnostics, not approved new contenders)
1. **Draw probability / calibration.** All four GW9 methods missed the two drawn fixtures by argmax. Review GW6–GW9 forecast probability assigned to draws, observed draw frequency, draw-specific log loss/Brier contributions, and uncertainty. Use frozen data only. Do not simply increase draw probabilities based on GW9.
2. **Model-versus-market disagreements and cold starts.** GW9 Coventry–Sunderland was won by Model 2 but not by market/blends; examine *all* disagreement and cold-start fixtures on the prospective matched set, with sample sizes and per-match contributions. Do not generalise from two GW9 cold starts.
3. **Modal 1-1 scoreline concentration.** Examine whether ten Model 2 1-1 modal predictions reflect reasonable Poisson mechanics, rounding or a feature/model issue; keep exact-score accuracy separate from 1X2 predictions.

**Experiment gate:** Diagnose first using existing frozen observations. If evidence motivates a new probabilistic method, write a falsifiable hypothesis, formula, minimum sample, prospective acceptance/guardrails and the intended pre-match release time **before** freezing its first shadow. Do not add arbitrary last-minute variants. Current recommendation is to carry forward the four unchanged approaches and avoid a new GW10 weight experiment.
