## Comparison and pre-match observations

- **10/10 identical 1X2 selections across all four approaches.** There are **no model-versus-market or model-versus-challenger argmax disagreements** this week. This does **not** mean the models are equivalent: their probability levels differ and can be compared objectively using post-match Log Loss and multiclass Brier.
- **No draw selected as the most likely overall 1X2 outcome** by any of the four approaches. The original **Model 2 modal score is `1-1` in 8/10** fixtures. Track draw-specific calibration separately and do not change frozen probabilities after observing results.
- **Cold starts:** Coventry City vs Crystal Palace; Tottenham vs Ipswich; Hull City vs Brighton (3/10), using the registered promoted-team translated-prior fallback. Compare these once final results exist; the sample is small.
- **Highest provisional champion win probability:** Hull City vs Brighton (**69.3%** for the selected 1X2 outcome). Lowest: Coventry City vs Crystal Palace (**41.7%**). A selection below 50% is still possible because the other two outcomes each have lower probability.
- **Freeze integrity:** all 10 FPL fixture IDs, dates, teams, market event IDs and kickoffs matched; saved H/D/A vectors are non-negative and sum to one within the specified tolerances; model hash and adaptive input hashes recorded. The freeze workflow passed all source and 24-hour checks.
- **No weights retrained or adjusted**, no future match results used, and no claim of improvement in the historical +2pp 1X2 target (fixed 40/60 historical baseline 54.00%, target ≥56.00%).
