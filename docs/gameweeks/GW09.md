### Diagnostic signals, not confirmed causes
- **Draws:** Both draws (Hull–Ipswich and Aston Villa–Fulham) were missed by all four most-likely 1X2 selections. A draw-calibration hypothesis is reasonable, but not proven.
- **Upsets:** All four missed Brighton's win at Man City and Everton's win at Newcastle. Avoid fitting to these two results.
- **Cold starts:** Model 2 correctly selected Coventry City over Sunderland, whereas the market/blends favoured Sunderland; Hull–Ipswich ended drawn and was missed by each approach. Two cold-start fixtures, insufficient for a general conclusion.
- **Model 2 modal score:** Its ten frozen modal scorelines were 1-1. Two final matches finished 1-1 or 2-2 (zero 1-1 exact matches). This remains a useful diagnostic, **not** evidence that all 1X2 picks were draws.
- **Ranking:** Model 2 wins GW9 probability scores, but fixed 40/60 retains the better *GW6–GW9 matched* aggregate. Neither observation alone justifies formal champion promotion or rollback.
