## A modelling distinction worth noting

The predicted outcome and the most likely exact score do not always agree.

For example, Football Copilot may give a team the highest overall probability of winning while identifying 1-1 as the single most likely exact scoreline.

This is not contradictory.

The win probability is the sum of all possible winning scorelines, while the exact-score prediction refers to only one score combination.

## Promoted-team cold start

The 2026/27 season created a modelling challenge because Coventry City and Hull City had no recent Premier League observations in the training dataset.

Rather than fabricate data, I built a promoted-team cold-start framework.

Historical Championship-to-Premier-League transitions were analysed across 12 promoted teams.

The selected prior combines:

**75% translated Championship performance**

with:

**25% historical Premier League baseline**

The 75/25 weighting produced the lowest combined error in the historical cold-start backtest.

Ipswich are also treated through the same framework to ensure consistent handling of all three promoted clubs.
